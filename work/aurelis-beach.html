Overview

Aurelis had photography most resorts would envy and a website that flattened all of it into a grid of rooms and prices.

We rebuilt the identity around light rather than logo. The palette shifts through the day, the typography stays quiet, and the site leads with what a stay actually feels like before it asks anyone to pick a date.

Booking sits one decision deep from every page, so guests never have to hunt for it — and never have to look at it before they want to.

Identity system and the booking flow

The brief

Sell the hour, not the room.

Guests do not remember a floor plan. They remember the walk down to the water at six, and the quiet after the restaurant closes. The brief was to make the site carry that, without tipping into the soft-focus language every resort already uses.

Challenge

Beautiful photography, buried three clicks down.

The old site opened on a booking widget. Everything that made the resort worth visiting sat behind a gallery link almost nobody clicked, and the rooms all photographed the same.

The booking engine was a third-party embed with its own styling, so the moment a guest committed, the brand fell away entirely.

Solution

One surface, from first look to confirmation.

We wrapped the booking engine in the resort's own interface, so rates and availability come from the same system as before while the experience never changes hands.

Room pages lead with the view from the room rather than the bed. Rates load quietly underneath, and the whole thing is served from the edge so the photography arrives before the guest scrolls.
